Slide 15 — "But Will the Leads Be Quality?"

How AI qualification ensures you only talk to serious buyers

Budget screening: The AI directly asks about budget and financing interest. If they say "just browsing" or refuse to discuss pricing, they're marked as unqualified—your team never sees them.
Timeline validation: The AI asks when they want to move forward. "Sometime next year" gets nurtured via email. "I want to book this month" gets priority booking.
Intent confirmation: The AI probes on why they're considering the procedure now. Generic curiosity gets filtered. Specific pain points or events ("wedding in 3 months") convert at 40%+.
Competitive intelligence: The AI asks if they're considering other practices. This data informs your competitive positioning and helps your team close more effectively.

Result: Your team only talks to people who have budget, urgency, and intent. Your close rate jumps from 22% to 35%+.
