The Evidence Behind Microsites + AI Intake

Why speed, structure, and signal-driven iteration produce measurable gains this quarter
What the numbers say:
  • The first responder wins the majority of cases; rapid follow-up within minutes radically increases conversion odds.
  • Even brand-new pilots show meaningful signals in week one (visibility, device gaps, quick technical fixes) you can act on immediately.
  • Our delivery model is built for measurable performance: sub-2s mobile loads, 5-field forms, and clear conversion targets from day one.

Dear Decision-Maker, if you've ever felt your marketing was "working hard but not working smart," the evidence you're about to see will clarify why—and what to do next.

Hard Evidence: Why Speed-to-Lead Is Your Weapon

  • First responder advantage: When you respond first, you capture the bulk of opportunities in competitive, high-intent markets.
  • Minutes matter: Responding within ~5 minutes dramatically lifts conversion odds; delays into the half-hour/hour range erase intent and depress outcomes.
  • The AI difference: Always-on outreach plus structured reminders increases captured appointments versus the "call tomorrow" approach.
"5-minute window" principle First responder wins the lion's share

Design That Delivers: What We Ship (and Why It Works)

Our microsites are engineered to convert intent, not just attract clicks.

  • Speed as a feature: Sub-2-second mobile loads, Lighthouse 90+ targets—because faster pages keep people engaged and lift conversions.
  • Frictionless capture: 5-field forms, sticky "Tap to Call," and consented follow-up that aligns ops with the on-page promise.
  • Local SEO signals: City/ZIP copy, LocalBusiness + FAQ schema, clean canonicals to support richer snippets and stable rankings.
  • Governed iteration: JSON content + CI/CD so copy, offers, and FAQs improve in minutes—not months.

Week-One Proof: What a Fresh Pilot Reveals (HVAC Example)

You don't need quarters of history to learn. In the first 7–14 days, your data already points to specific, high-leverage fixes.

Signals observed (brand-new pilot):

  • Visibility exists: 39 impressions recorded with average position ~32 across variants—small but actionable confirmation you're eligible and showing.
  • Device gap exposed: Desktop avg. position ~12.5 vs mobile ~43—prioritize mobile UX/speed to close the gap where local demand actually lives.
  • Technical duplication: Both http and https versions indexed for the same city page—consolidate under HTTPS + canonical to stabilize performance.
  • Near-term upside: A high-intent query touched page-one range (positions ~10.5 → 9 on different days). Rewrite title/meta and on-page sections to win that click first.

What Happens When You Pair Speed With Structure

  • You earn the click: Device-specific titles/meta mapped to the exact high-intent phrases already surfacing in Search Console.
  • You capture the lead: Mobile-first UX and immediate, helpful follow-up within the critical window.
  • You keep the appointment: Confirmations and reminders reduce no-shows, so calendars reflect real revenue, not wishful thinking.

Economics You Can Defend

Field Notes: Why "Phone-Only" Leaves Money on the Table

When the only option is "call us," intent decays. People compare multiple providers and default to whoever makes the next step obvious.

  • Faster first touch wins: A responsive, instrumented request-a-consult path + instant callback protects the 5-minute window.
  • Structured FAQs matter: Price/financing and timeline answers reduce friction and raise show rates (and trust).

Proof Stack (What You'll See on the Dashboard)

  • Acquisition: Impressions, position by device, and CTR for top queries (pinpoint titles/meta to fix next).
  • Behavior: phone_click, form_submit, and completion rates tied to mobile UX changes.
  • Operations: Time-to-first-response and coverage against your promised SLA (the "trust gap" tracker).
