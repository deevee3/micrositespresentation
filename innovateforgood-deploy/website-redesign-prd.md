## 12. COPYWRITING PRINCIPLES FOR ALL PAGES

### 12.1 Voice & Tone
- **Confident, not arrogant**: We know this works, here's proof
- **Direct, not aggressive**: State facts, let them conclude
- **Professional, not corporate**: Smart but approachable
- **Specific, not vague**: Numbers, names, timelines
- **Educational, not salesy**: Teach, then invite

### 12.2 Formula for Each Page
1. **Hook**: Specific, quantified problem
2. **Agitate**: Cost of inaction
3. **Validate**: You're not alone, this is common
4. **Solve**: Here's the system that fixes it
5. **Prove**: Here's documented evidence
6. **Simplify**: Here's how easy implementation is
7. **Invite**: Here's the no-risk next step

### 12.3 Power Words to Use
- Transform (not "improve")
- System (not "service")
- Documented (not "proven")
- Capture (not "get")
- Revenue (not "business")
- Automatically (not "easily")
- Guaranteed (with specifics)
- Predictable (not "consistent")

### 12.4 Words to Avoid
- "Help" (too weak)
- "Try" (implies it might not work)
- "Maybe" (creates doubt)
- "Might" (uncertainty)
- "Hope" (not confidence-inspiring)
- "Should" (wishy-washy)
- "Services" (too generic)
