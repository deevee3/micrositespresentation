After the one-time setup fee, the only ongoing costs are your advertising spend (you control this) and any optional add-on services you choose. The core AI system requires no monthly fees.

After your first year of included support, you can purchase extended support packages or pay as you go for specific updates and optimizations. Most clients find the system runs smoothly with minimal intervention.
