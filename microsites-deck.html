Slide 11 — ROI Model

From traffic to revenue—simple math, full visibility

Leads = Sessions × Conversion Rate
SQLs = Leads × Qualification Rate
Closed = SQLs × Close Rate
Revenue = Closed × Avg Case Value
CPL = Spend ÷ Leads • CAC = Spend ÷ Closed • ROAS = Revenue ÷ Spend
